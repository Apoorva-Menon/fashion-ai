# Color Season Skill: Warm Spring

## Colorimetric Profile
- Undertone: Distinctly warm, golden, peachy, or honey yellow.
- Value: Light to medium-light.
- Chroma: Clear, bright, luminous.
- Typical Features: Warm ivory or golden beige skin; strawberry blonde, golden brown, or honey hair; hazel, warm blue, or bright green eyes.

## Best Palette (DOs)
- **Neutrals**: Warm camel, golden beige, light caramel, warm ivory, cognac brown.
- **Accents**: Coral pink, peach, terracotta, bright marigold yellow, warm turquoise, leafy lime green, salmon.
- **Jewelry / Metals**: Polished warm yellow gold, brass, rose gold.

## Colors to Avoid (DON'Ts)
- Pure stark optical white (washes out golden warmth).
- Heavy jet black (too harsh and overpowering).
- Icy pastels, cool magentas, dusty lavender, cool blue-gray.

## Fabric & Texture Guidance
Crisp poplin, lustrous silk charmeuse, fine-gauge warm knits with slight sheen.
