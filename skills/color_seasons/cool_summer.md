# Color Season Skill: Cool Summer

## Colorimetric Profile
- Undertone: Cool, pink, blue, or delicate porcelain rose.
- Value: Medium-light to medium.
- Chroma: Soft, muted, dusted, smoky.
- Typical Features: Cool rosy skin; ash blonde, mushroom brown, or ash brown hair; soft gray-blue, cool hazel, or slate green eyes.

## Best Palette (DOs)
- **Neutrals**: Slate gray, soft navy, rose beige, soft pewter, off-white / pearl white.
- **Accents**: Lavender, dusty rose, powder blue, sage green, cool mauve, raspberry, soft plum.
- **Jewelry / Metals**: Polished silver, platinum, white gold, brushed pewter.

## Colors to Avoid (DON'Ts)
- Warm golden tones: mustard yellow, orange, terracotta, warm olive.
- Harsh high-contrast pure black and bright fluorescent neons.

## Fabric & Texture Guidance
Matte jersey, soft cashmere, linen, brushed cotton, sueded textures that diffuse light gently.
