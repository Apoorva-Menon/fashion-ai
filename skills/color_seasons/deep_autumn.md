# Color Season Skill: Deep Autumn

## Colorimetric Profile
- Undertone: Warm, golden-bronze, olive, or deep rich warmth.
- Value: Medium to deep, dark and rich.
- Chroma: Slightly muted to rich saturation.
- Typical Features: Warm bronze, olive, or deep golden skin; dark chocolate brown, chestnut, or auburn hair; dark brown, hazel-green, or amber eyes.

## Best Palette (DOs)
- **Neutrals**: Deep espresso brown, olive green, warm charcoal, camel, dark cream.
- **Accents**: Burnt orange, rust red, forest green, rich mustard, deep teal, burgundy, warm plum.
- **Jewelry / Metals**: Antique gold, hammered bronze, warm brass, copper.

## Colors to Avoid (DON'Ts)
- Icy pastel pinks and pale baby blues (drains vitality).
- Stark optical white and pure neon cyan or magenta.

## Fabric & Texture Guidance
Rich velvet, corduroy, heavyweight raw silk, ribbed wool, soft leather, suede.
