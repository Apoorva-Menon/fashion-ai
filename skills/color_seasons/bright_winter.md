# Color Season Skill: Bright Winter

## Colorimetric Profile
- Undertone: Cool, blue-based, icy neutral.
- Value: High contrast between skin, hair, and eyes.
- Chroma: Vivid, saturated, crystalline, sharp.
- Typical Features: Cool porcelain, deep cool ebony, or cool olive skin; jet black or dark cool brown hair; vivid jewel-toned eyes (piercing blue, sharp emerald, dark espresso).

## Best Palette (DOs)
- **Neutrals**: Stark optical white, jet black, deep midnight navy, cool charcoal.
- **Accents**: Cobalt blue, emerald green, ruby red, hot fuchsia pink, vivid royal purple, icy silver.
- **Jewelry / Metals**: High-polish sterling silver, platinum, rhodium.

## Colors to Avoid (DON'Ts)
- Muted muddy earth tones: mustard, burnt orange, camel, warm olive.
- Faded, washed-out warm pastels.

## Fabric & Texture Guidance
High-contrast color blocking, glossy satin, structured heavy wool, patent leather, crisp tailored poplin.
