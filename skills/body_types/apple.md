# Body Type Skill: Apple (Round / Oval)

## Morphological Characteristics
- Upper body and torso are prominent, carrying weight predominantly around midsection and chest.
- Shoulders and bust are average to broad.
- Waist is undefined or the widest section of the body.
- Legs and arms are often noticeably slender and shapely.

## What Suits Best (DOs)
### Fabrics
- Softly draping, matte fabrics that skim the midsection without clinging or bunching: matte silk, soft jersey, georgette, light fine knitwear.
- Flowing, non-rigid vertical layers.

### Garment Types & Silhouettes
- **Tops**: Deep V-necks, wrap tunics, empire waist tops, flowy asymmetrical hemlines, tops that hit below hip crest.
- **Bottoms**: Straight-leg, bootcut, or flared trousers that showcase slender legs; flat-front waistbands without pleats.
- **Dresses**: Empire waist dresses, A-line shift dresses that float away from the waist, wrap dresses with gentle drapes.
- **Outerwear**: Long open-front cardigans, tailored dusters, unbuttoned single-breasted coats creating strong vertical parallel lines.

## What to Avoid (DON'Ts)
- Tight, clingy tops or high-necked tight turtlenecks that amplify torso volume.
- Belts cinched tightly across the midsection.
- Horizontal stripes across the abdomen, crop tops, or stiff high-rise pleated trousers.
- Ruffles or tiered embellishments across the stomach.

## Styling Narrative & Subtitle Direction
Draw focus to your best assets—gorgeous legs, neckline, and collarbone—while creating unbroken vertical elegance down the torso.
