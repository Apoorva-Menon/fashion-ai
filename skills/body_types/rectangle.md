# Body Type Skill: Rectangle (Athletic / Column)

## Morphological Characteristics
- Bust, waist, and hips share roughly similar widths.
- Waist is straight with subtle or minimal indentation.
- Long limbs, athletic or lean silhouette, flatter buttocks.
- Shoulders and hips are well aligned.

## What Suits Best (DOs)
### Fabrics
- Fabrics with texture, drape, and stiffness to construct dimension: linen, twill, ribbed knits, structured wool, leather.
- Color blocking and contrasting patterns to define zones.

### Garment Types & Silhouettes
- **Tops**: Ruffled tops, peplum blouses, sweetheart or plunging necklines, breast pocket details.
- **Bottoms**: High-waisted paperbag trousers, belted tapered pants, pleated palazzo pants, cargo styles.
- **Dresses**: Belted shirt dresses, bias-cut silk slip dresses, cut-out dresses creating illusion curves.
- **Outerwear**: Belted trenches, double-breasted blazers with waist cinch, cropped bomber jackets.

## What to Avoid (DON'Ts)
- Straight, vertical shift dresses or tubular boxy silhouettes with zero waist definition (looks stiff and square).
- Low-rise shapeless jeans that elongate the torso and flatten the hips.
- Monochromatic, clingy tube tops with unbelted straight trousers.

## Styling Narrative & Subtitle Direction
Create curves and dynamic focal points by cinching the waist and introducing architectural volume at the shoulders and hips simultaneously.
