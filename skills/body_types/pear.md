# Body Type Skill: Pear (Triangle)

## Morphological Characteristics
- Hips and thighs wider than shoulders and bust.
- Well-defined waist, often high-waisted.
- Slender neck, narrower or sloping shoulders, smaller bust.
- Weight carries predominantly in lower body (hips, buttocks, thighs).

## What Suits Best (DOs)
### Fabrics
- Structured fabrics for upper body (crisp cotton, structured tweed, brocade) to create shoulder architecture.
- Fluid, draped fabrics for lower body (viscose, fine wool, crepe, dark stretch denim) that skim rather than cling.

### Garment Types & Silhouettes
- **Tops**: Boatneck, bardot/off-shoulder, cowl neck, flutter sleeves, puff shoulders, horizontal stripes on top.
- **Bottoms**: Dark-wash straight-leg or A-line skirts, tailored wide-leg trousers starting cleanly from the hip, high-waist palazzo pants.
- **Dresses**: A-line dresses, empire cut, wrap dresses with fluttering sleeves.
- **Outerwear**: Cropped jackets hitting above the widest hip line, or mid-thigh coats with shoulder pads and wide lapels.

## What to Avoid (DON'Ts)
- Light-colored or patterned skinny pants and clingy pencil skirts that over-emphasize lower body width.
- Tops that hem directly across the widest point of the hips/thighs.
- Cargo pants with side hip pockets, pleats directly over hips, or heavy hip embellishments.
- Drop-waist garments and shapeless shift dresses.

## Styling Narrative & Subtitle Direction
Draw visual interest upward toward the shoulders, neck, and collarbone, while allowing the lower body line to flow smoothly without interruption.
