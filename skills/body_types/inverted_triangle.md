# Body Type Skill: Inverted Triangle

## Morphological Characteristics
- Shoulders noticeably broader than hips; strong, athletic upper frame.
- Bust can be average to full; ribcage is often broad.
- Waist is subtly defined with little curve.
- Hips are narrow and legs are typically slender and shapely.

## What Suits Best (DOs)
### Fabrics
- Soft, flowing fabrics for the upper body (chiffon, silk, rayon) to soften broad shoulders.
- Textures, prints, and heavier structured fabrics for the lower body (pleated fabrics, jacquard, rigid denim, corduroy).

### Garment Types & Silhouettes
- **Tops**: Raglan sleeves, deep V-necks, scoop necks, halter necks, vertical neckline lines.
- **Bottoms**: Cargo pants, wide-leg trousers, boyfriend or flared jeans, pleated skirts, full circle skirts that create hip volume.
- **Dresses**: Fit-and-flare, peplum skirts, drop-waist dresses that draw attention downward.
- **Outerwear**: Collarless jackets, long duster coats, unpadded single-breasted coats that create vertical length.

## What to Avoid (DON'Ts)
- Shoulder pads, epaulets, puff sleeves, boatnecks, and off-shoulder bardot tops.
- Tight skinny jeans paired with oversized, boxy, or shoulder-accentuating tops (exaggerates top-heaviness).
- Horizontal stripes across the chest and shoulders.

## Styling Narrative & Subtitle Direction
Soften and lengthen the upper body while adding volume, texture, and eye-catching movement to the hips and lower body to restore visual equilibrium.
